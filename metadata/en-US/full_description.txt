What's new in version 1.20.0
* Accent color: the "Toward black" slider is replaced by two sliders, Saturation and Brightness. Pastel, beige or brown tones are now possible, and the swatches update to preview the result.
* Text on the accent color switches between white and black for the best contrast.
* Small screens: spacing tightens automatically to avoid needless scrolling; the time format now fits on a single line.

Turn your phone into a simple night light, with no ads and no trackers.

Features:
* Two light sources: the camera flash, or the screen with an adjustable warm tint.
* Adjustable duration before automatic shutdown (1 to 120 minutes).
* Instant or progressive (fade) shutdown, with adjustable fade duration.
* Fully pure-black (OLED) interface, to save battery and avoid glare.
* A tap brings back the controls (remaining time, Stop button).
* Once the timer ends, the app closes itself so the phone can go to sleep normally.

Settings screen:
* Time display format: compact, detailed, or HH:MM:SS.
* UI accent color: palette of hues and white, with adjustable saturation and brightness (pastel, brown...).
* App language: 26 languages available, independent of the system language.

No Internet permission, no telemetry, fully free software (GPL-3.0).
